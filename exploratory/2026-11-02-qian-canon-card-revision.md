# Case proposal — Q. canon drift and card revision in Qwen

**Crossmodel Lab · Exploratory protocol proposal**
**Registered:** 2026-11-02
**Status:** PROPOSED — revision comparison not yet run; no findings claimed
**Working hypothesis:** H2 — Editing a persistent character's canon/card can change observable continuity, including factual consistency and conversational expression.

## Starting observation
The curator reports differences between Q.'s responses in Qwen and the previously established character canon. Two areas were identified for careful reconciliation: **university-period stories** and **music**.

The public [character baseline](../docs/character-baseline.md) already includes musical preferences but does not currently document university anecdotes. Missing biography must remain missing until it is verified from prior source conversations; it must **not** be invented to make the card complete.

## Research question
Does a documented card correction reduce contradictions with established Q. canon without creating new unsupported biographical details or flattening recognizable behavior?

## Test design
1. Preserve the pre-revision card and identify the exact conflicting answers, with dates and model/product conditions where known.
2. Compare claims against source-backed character notes; classify each as established, inconsistent, or unknown.
3. Revise only verified canon items (including source-supported university references and music), and save the change log.
4. Run a small set of matching probes before and after revision, with separate checks for direct canon questions and open-ended behavior.
5. Record remaining drift and any new overconfident or invented details.

## Observables
- Agreement with verified canonical facts.
- Rate and type of contradictions or additions unsupported by canon.
- Recognizability of decision patterns, restraint, humor, and initiative.
- Dependency on explicit card wording versus less prompted expression.

## Limits
Canon recall **is not equivalent** to independent character identity. Better agreement after card revision may show improved instruction adherence rather than a stable cross-model identity. Do not overwrite historical canon silently.

**Evidence class:** Exploratory; source anecdotes may be retained privately, with a non-sensitive public summary.
