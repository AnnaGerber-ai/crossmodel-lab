Only videos that contribute to continuity, multimodal, or behavioral observations are listed here.

## 2026-09-17 — Grok motion tests

**Type:** exploratory identity-continuity tests  
**Subject:** Q.  
**Method:** image-to-video generation in Grok  
**Purpose:** evaluate whether facial identity remains stable when still references are animated

**Observation:**  
The motion tests preserved the general identity and visual character of Q., but some facial drift remained visible during animation.

The results were useful as exploratory multimodal references, but were not treated as controlled visual-continuity tests.

**Status:** exploratory
