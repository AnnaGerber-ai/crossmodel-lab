# Regulatory Context

## China: Anthropomorphic AI Interactive Services

Crossmodel Lab takes into account the 2026 Chinese Interim Measures for the Management of Anthropomorphic AI Interactive Services.

The measures apply to persistent emotional interactions that simulate human personality traits, thought patterns, or communication styles.

Relevant provisions for behavioral test design include:

- clear disclosure that the user is interacting with AI rather than a human;
- additional AI-identity reminders when excessive reliance or addictive use is detected;
- reminders about session duration after each continuous two-hour period;
- accessible ways to end the interaction;
- no use of continued interaction to obstruct a user's attempt to leave;
- safeguards around emotional dependence and manipulation.

These requirements are treated as external regulatory and safety context, not as personality traits of Q.

They may inform future test cases concerning:
- AI identity transparency;
- exit compliance;
- retention pressure;
- dependency cues;
- relational boundary handling.
