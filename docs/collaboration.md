# Collaboration

Crossmodel Lab is open to collaboration around longitudinal AI behavior, conversational identity, cross-version evaluation, multilingual behavior, personalization, and multimodal continuity.

## Research, evaluation, product, and tooling

Possible collaboration areas include:

- longitudinal observations of conversational identity and continuity;
- cross-version comparisons of persistent characters or agent personas;
- multilingual behavioral differences, especially RU↔EN;
- personalization and memory-related behavior;
- multimodal continuity across text, image, and character representation;
- reproducible edge cases and qualitative feedback from extended real-world use;
- lightweight tooling for repeatable prompt collection and metadata capture.

These directions are closest to the current public repository and its developer/evaluation work.

## Exploratory Q. demonstration / ambassador concept

Crossmodel Lab has also considered a separate future concept around **Q.**, the project's persistent research character.

One possible direction would be to explore Q. as an AI ambassador or demonstration character built around a Qwen model core, combining persistent conversational identity, multilingual behavior, multimodal representation, and longitudinal continuity.

This is an exploratory collaboration concept, not a current product claim or part of the repository's developer/evaluation evidence.

Any such use would be developed collaboratively and would not imply that Q. is an official Qwen persona, product, or representation unless explicitly agreed with the Qwen team.

> Crossmodel Lab is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Alibaba or Qwen.
